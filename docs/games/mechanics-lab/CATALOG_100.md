# Catalog of 100 game scenarios

Updated October 8, 2026. Working page: `/lab/missions`. These are simplified game prototypes for user selection; Graphics are not a criterion for this game.

All 33 scripts identified by the audit as predominantly text-based have been reworked. After corrections based on reviews, they have a persistent world: 19 marshalling yards, 9 assembly lines, 3 field investigations, 1 English room and 1 baggage office. Single, presentation, and local collaboration formats are available for these scenarios. Below is the original educational material of the catalogue; The actual game cycles of the remade missions are described in the section “Reworking 33 Scenarios”.

## What counts as a script?

The catalog contains 6 original missions with separate rules and 94 new missions. The 61 new missions retain three training stages with a total supply. An additional 33 missions have been reworked into continuous gameplay loops, described below. The order of combinations of mechanics in 94 missions is not repeated. 12 common mechanics are used; a hundred scenarios does not mean a hundred unique game systems. Eight short exercise machines `/lab/mechanics` are not included in this number.

## Original Cycle: Currently used in 61 missions

1. The student selects a subject or searches for a topic, reads the goal, the three steps, and the known event. The “Start Mission” button opens the first stage.
2. Using actions on the field, the student builds a solution. The Try Solution button checks the constraints and explains the error. A failed check does not take away the stock or reset the structure.
3. The right decision uses up the stock. “Apply result and continue” carries the remainder to the next step. “Improve this decision” returns the cost of the decision made and opens the field again.
4. Before the third stage, the event consumes 3 units. Its limitation is indicated in the stage conditions. “Revise previous step” returns its cost; when exiting the final stage, it also returns the cost of the event. Re-entry applies the expense again, without accumulating free stock.
5. After three decisions have been made, the “Complete Mission” button opens the result with the remaining stock, the number of checks and the use of hints. You can replay or return to the catalog.

Starting stock - 18. Price of the accepted stage `max(1, ceil(volume / 3))`. Volume: length of the route/program/sequence, number of sets taken, number of distributed cards, sum of coefficients, number of experiments, perimeter of the site, cost of the network, completion time of the last work, number of transformations or sum of prices of included sources. This is a conditional game resource, not a physical quantity. Before the last stage, flow rate 3 is added.

The main connection between the stages of the 61 original missions is the budget and the thematic scenario. The completed form, text, or route does not automatically become input to the next step. Such a deepening is provided after selection; more meaningful branches already exist in the six original missions.

## Management and training inspection

| Mechanics | Student Actions | What is being checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route | Add N/E/S/W, delete any command; the actual path is visible on the field | Goal, obligatory points, obstacles, field edges, limit |
| Equipment | Take/return sets using the +/− buttons | Accurate composition based on several indicators and warehouse balances |
| Sequence | Click fragments in the desired order, remove selected ones | Author's acceptable order; for language - options specified in the script |
| Distribution | Select a category for each card | All categories, explanation of the first error |
| Balancing | Change coefficients before formulas | Preserving each element, minimum positive integer coefficients |
| Conveyor | Add/remove modules; see results on multiple inputs | All control inputs, program length, order of operations |
| Experiment | Select two quantities, conduct an experiment, compare a log, enter a forecast | At least two experiments with one changing quantity and a correct forecast; model boundaries are indicated |
| Plot | Turn cells on/off | Area, perimeter, connectedness by sides, obligatory and forbidden cells |
| Network | Enable/disable connections on the diagram | Channel availability, connectivity to node 0, total budget |
| Schedule | Indicate the beginning of each work on the scale | Duration, dependencies, busy intervals, no overlaps |
| Conversion | Move, rotate and reflect a piece, cancel a move | Contour matching, field boundaries and number of moves |
| Proof | Open sources, include/exclude them from argument | The sources I have read support all parts of the statement; no irrelevant ones, budget adhered to |

Instructional models are explicitly identified in the conditions. For example, a network of controllers is not presented as an electrical circuit, a geometric fastening is not presented as a simulation of rays, and a conditional linear heating model is not presented as a universal law. In mass fraction, the mass of the substance does not exceed the mass of the solution. Historical evidence is clearly used as teaching cases.

## Rework of 33 scenarios

Implemented after the request to remove text switching. The first three general systems, after reviews, are supplemented by a subject English room and a luggage office. These are 33 scenarios on common systems, not 33 new unique engines. The catalog has a filter “Updated games with a field (33)”. A review of Wordwall and other guidelines, initial feedback, corrections and limitations are recorded in the [plan](../../ISLAND_GAMES_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#review-of-ready-solutions-before-corrections--October-9).

- **Station:** the student recognizes the contents of the cargo and programs the path to the desired port. The cart actually moves through the cells; Wrong port does not accept cargo. The constructed paths are saved and do not consume charge on subsequent trips. After two deliveries, the center closes: a detour or repairs are needed near the cart. Unloading does not teleport the player; an empty cart can be returned with one command along a familiar path. The following cargo can also be sent via an already built network with one command, choosing the port yourself. In the census, after six entries, the birds' unique marks must be counted and a zero sighting must be distinguished from a miss in order to place a backup camera.
- **Line:** documents confirm the input conditions, then the student constructs the order of operations and distributes the limited command. The product passes the station; a causal error stops him from taking a specific action. The fix preserves a valid executed prefix. The second release uses the same design, but one station requires two workers: the team needs to be redistributed. In the three remaining language scenarios, operations are replaced by meaningful procedures rather than individual words of a sentence.
- **Investigation:** documents are located in different archives on the map. The student chooses a place; The character fits automatically, the bag holds two documents. The found document must be delivered and then associated with a specific fact. The check is carried out on individual connections and takes into account the budget of the sources. Then you need to choose a reasonable conclusion: changing the location of the opening does not determine the location of the next meeting; a copy of the diary does not become second independent evidence; the originator of the entry is not necessarily the author of the quotation. The signed file is delivered to the editor, historical council or publishing house. If the output is incorrect, the documents are saved for correction.
- **English room:** the guest's request determines the actions with objects. The student chooses a number, approaches the objects, opens a window or cabinet, carries mugs, connects and turns on the lamp, and clears the passage. The guest walks to the table and reacts to the state of the room. There are several acceptable night plans; In the morning you need to prepare a video call: first ventilate, then close the street window, free the laptop socket occupied by the lamp and put the drink on another table. A successful call test must be repeated after changing the room. Translation and clarifications are available upon request. Audio, free input of lines and a big world are not yet available.

- **Baggage Bureau:** the student takes the suitcase from the belt, inspects the wheels and sticker, checks the owner’s information, compares evidence. Observations are stored on a shelf. After the first delivery, the tags of similar suitcases are mixed, so the item needs to be checked and the name corrected. The home continuation changes the procedure: the sealed gift is checked from the outside, and the owner and the receiving friend are listed separately. There are three formats and mobile workplace tabs.

In the three common systems, the single player format automatically plays out the challenge. Presentation offers frame-by-frame display, automatic full display and pause. In the room and baggage desk, a common screen allows you to show step by step the guest's approach and the issue of luggage. On the mobile screen during the test, the field and display buttons are available together. This is a controlled show on one screen, without a separate presenter application.

In a co-op format, roles are reversed on the same screen. At the station, the partner sees the port code table and controls the repair passage, the student sees the cargo and sets the route. In an investigation, the student collects documents, the partner reads their contents and issues clearances; The student connects evidence to facts. In the workshop, the partner prepares and approves the input documents, the student builds the line. Without transfer of admission, the joint inspection does not take place. Technically, this mode does not attempt to hide information from the owner of the shared screen; There is no online game.

The positions of objects, rails, loads, documents, evidentiary connections, line, workers, display and format are preserved. Restarting one mission does not affect avatars, skins or other games. Old attempts of the original version are not deleted; The new rules are being passed separately.

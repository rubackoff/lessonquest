# Roblox: Guidelines for Training Templates

The student is playing. A short start of three tasks takes place with a tutor, the continuation is at home. Skins can later be unlocked through training achievements. The editor is not yet being developed.

We look at [Playing Now](https://www.roblox.com/charts/top-playing-now), and not total visits. In [official request](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7326934954,383310974,994732206,1686885941) October 7, 2026: Brookhaven 313 603, Adopt Me! 167,407, 99 Nights 145,770, Blox Fruits 145,519. These are selected popular landmarks and a changing cross-section, not a complete ranking.

| Landmark | What to adapt | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Steal a Brainrot](https://www.roblox.com/games/109983668079237/Steal-a-Brainrot) | Purchase, collection with income, theft from the bot's base, lock through the task, pursuit to your base | Working draft "Character Hunt" |
| [99 Nights in the Forest](https://www.roblox.com/games/79546208627805/99-Nights-in-the-Forest) | Survival, exploration, defense and camp development | Previous draft removed: moving crates did not preserve core mechanics |
| [Adopt Me!](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237/Adopt-Me) | Pet development for mastered exercises | Candidate |
| Blox Fruits | Training quests and opening zones by mastering the topic | Candidate |
| Brookhaven | Role-playing dialogues and everyday tasks in the city | Candidate |

High online is a reason to study the cycle, not proof of its effectiveness for learning. Testing short rounds with students. We do not copy other people's brands, cards and assets.

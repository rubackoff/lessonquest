# Forest Camp - Visual Specification

`concept-v1.png` - the selected working concept of a full screen 1536 × 1024. The existing light skin of the games continues with common real 3D avatars. Hierarchy: white navigation → study question with three options → large playing field → control and explicit start.

Colors: white `#ffffff`, text `#173b31`, secondary `#58756a`, accent `#288264`, needles `#3b6952`, moss `#8caf88`, bonfire `#f2bb65`. Font Onest, main question 36–40 px, heading 28 px, answers 24 px, interface 16 px. Control radii are 12 px, without additional cards around the field.

Controls: arrows/WASD and four buttons; E and separate interaction button; pause; shared wardrobe. Options above - legend for loads A/B/C. They are not responsible for the student. The Reading, Carrying, Dismantling, Camp Development, Night, and Summary states use the same screen and simple dialogue.

Mobile version: the whole question and three answers above the field; the camera shows all three loads; buttons at least 44 px; the instruction is shortened without changing the meaning; the screen can scroll vertically. Enlarging the camera should not flatten the hero.

Intentional difference from the bitmap concept: the character, boxes, trees, fire and shelter are real mesh objects. Free movement, load carrying, lighting at night and general approved GLBs require a three-dimensional stage. Clothing and animations are not regenerated. From ImageGen, separate distant forest background and soil texture are used; The HUD, responses and controls remain HTML. The tent in the concept shows the purpose of development: on the first day of the game, before purchase, only its foundation is visible, after purchase, a shelter appears.

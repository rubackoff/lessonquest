1. **Hoodie:** turquoise loose hoodie, dark cargo, light chunky sneakers, cut brown hair, backpack. We save the previously approved common avatar.
2. **Hook:** source of inspiration - [Pudge from Dota 2](https://www.dota2.com/hero/pudge?l=russian). Chosen as one of the most recognizable heroes, not as the winner of a non-existent objective recognition ranking; its historical popularity is confirmed by [PC Gamer's story on a billion matches](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/moba/dota-2s-pudge-becomes-the-mobas-first-character-to-be-played-in-over-a-billion-matches/). The visual ideas of a voluminous silhouette, an apron, a chain, a hook and green hair color were borrowed. Individual costume: brick-red quilted volume, light apron with leather patch, asymmetrical light shoulder pad, belts, bracers and rounded hook accessory. The human face, hair geometry and basic anatomy of our avatar are preserved; hair is dyed green. We do not tolerate Pooja's body, wounds, stitches on the skin, blood, a cleaver or the exact layout of the original costume.
3. **Phantom:** Inspiration: [Simon "Ghost" Riley from Call of Duty, Activision Official Material](https://newsroom.activisionblizzard.com/p/call-of-duty-ghost-valentine). The skull motif balaclava, headset and tactical silhouette are borrowed. Self-contained costume: own one-piece angular mask with two separate dark eye sockets on top of fabric, graphite clothing, sand panels, light scarf, offset pouches, knee pads, backpack and small turquoise details. No Scream mask, cape, Ghost mask replica, Task Force 141 patches, logos, flags, weapons or camo replicas.

## What is considered compliance during implementation?

- One basic anatomy and compatible skeleton for three costumes; clothing, masks and accessories vary.
- The Hook suit's apron/shoulder/chain and the Phantom's balaclava/headset/vest are distinguishable when scaled down to character size in the maze. It's not enough to repaint an old model.
- Phantom's dark clothing has visible graphite layers and does not require the interface or level to be darkened.
- First check the real model from the front, side, back and running next to the concept; then check the selection and saving of the skin from the office and the appearance of the same suit in the maze.
- The apron, chain, hook and pouches do not hide your footwork or cross your arms when running. Check the size and fastening of accessories separately on a real model.
- Do not replace implementation screenshots with concept art and do not declare compliance before visual inspection.

# Character Hunt - Working Draft

Landmark - [Steal a Brainrot](https://www.roblox.com/games/109983668079237/Steal-a-Brainrot). We keep recognizable actions: buy a character, receive income from the base, pick up a rare opponent from the base, run away with him to a safe zone. The rules for purchasing, income, locks and slowing down when stealing are also described on the page [official browser version](https://poki.com/en/g/steal-a-brainrot).

7 October 2026 official [Roblox Games API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486) returned 79,955 concurrent players. This is a changing cross-section, not a claim of first place in the rankings.

## Start with a tutor

The student always plays. The tutor chooses a training topic and helps discuss mistakes. The goal of the short round is to collect three characters by solving three tasks. The estimated duration is a few minutes, without the mandatory twenty-minute wait.

1. The student goes to the center tape, presses E. The game stops to read the task. The correct answer opens a purchase for 10 coins. Foxbot immediately appears at his base and begins to bring coins.
2. At the Red base, the student solves the following task: the laser lock is turned off. He takes Capicot and runs to the green base. The speed with the character is lower; the guard starts the chase. Delivery is counted upon entering your base.
3. Drakosha is similarly taken from the blue base. After the third character, the round ends: the result of the tasks, repetition of mistakes, link to continue at home.

A mistake in the task does not waste money and does not start a chase. You need to read the explanation and solve it again. The first attempt is saved separately from mastering the task. If the guard catches up, the character returns to the opponent; The lock that has already been solved remains open; there is no need to answer again for a new attempt to escape.

Characters bring 1/2/4 coins per second of game time. For 8 coins, a student can remotely turn on the protection of his base for 12 seconds: the guard will stop as he approaches it. Protection is not available until the first purchase, so as not to spend the starting capital without a source of income.

## DZ

The link continues the training set after the first three tasks: in the demo there are seven more. The same mechanics are repeated with purchases and two bases. For completed tasks, a general achievement is issued in your personal account; unlocking skins for achievements remains the next step. Progress of knowledge and achievements are local, in this browser; the game collection starts anew in each round.

## Draft boundaries

One arena, one bot guard, three original types of collectible characters. There is no multiplayer, theft of the student's collection by other players, rebirth or item store. AI generator and editor not added: the template accepts a ready-made training set with questions and answer options.

The former "Forest Camp" template has been removed: moving answers in boxes did not preserve the game content of the reference. The old URL redirects to the new game while maintaining the theme and DZ mode.

# Visual draft

Full screen: [concept-v1.png](concept-v1.png). White Onest Interface, Mint Secure Base Below, Center Ribbon, Cream Open Bases Red and Blue, Laser Locks, Large Hero. The task appears in a modal window directly upon purchase/theft; while reading, the world stands still.

Game scene - native Three.js. The hero and guard use real generic GLBs: hoodie/selected skin and phantom. The size and animation of the approved models did not change. The camera follows the hero, is lower and closer to the previous general view of the camp. The mini-map maintains orientation on a narrow screen.

Intentional simplifications for the requested working draft: simple open buildings and site instead of detailed concept architecture; collectible characters - transparent hand-drawn 3D renderings on the billboard, not new rigged models; purchase price 10 coins; one bot; a plain white header and bottom buttons instead of a world overlay interface. The concept is not used as a flat game background.

Real desktop/mobile images and interactive checks are saved outside the source code. Do not pass off the concept as a snapshot of a working game.

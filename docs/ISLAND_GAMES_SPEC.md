# Mechanics and scenarios of the four island games

Agreed concept of October 8, 2026. The document specifies the target behavior; The presence of an item here does not mean that it has already been implemented. The queue, statuses and completion criteria are maintained in the [development plan](ISLAND_GAMES_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

Games: base defense, expedition with construction, investigation, joint adventure with repair or escape. The learning goal determines the activities and content. The student plays and decides; the tutor helps or gets a role with other information. Timings and balance are preliminary and will be clarified after playthroughs.

## Shared world and audience

One character with a saved skin and pets moves between locations. The central base contains a collection of achievements and unlocked cosmetic items. Defense takes place near the base, construction takes place in gorges and workshops, investigation takes place in the port and city, and joint missions take place in the lighthouse, observatory, and stations.

| Location | Topics | Game Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crystal Gorge | Angles, symmetry, turns | Set up beams and mechanisms |
| Air Islands | Triangles, lengths, similarity | Calculate and build crossings |
| Architects' Quarter | Area, perimeter, volume | Design the site and structures |
| Mechanism City | Expressions, equations, systems | Set up lifts and flows |
| Observatory | Coordinates, graphs, functions | Recover dependencies and trajectories |
| Travelers Port | English questions, directions, time | Find out the route and complete the assignment |
| Trade area | Shopping, descriptions, comparisons | Confirm your order and select items |
| Clock City | Tenses and Sequence | Restore events |
| Ancient ruins | Logic, evidence | Check versions and open path |

For grades 5–8, emphasis on strategy, independent choice, and visible change in the world. For 9–11 - more complex content and quick entry into practice. For adult English - natural dialogues and a meaningful story. Age and gender do not limit the games available; Difficulty and presentation are selected separately. The educational vertical tape has been put aside.

During the lesson, one mission lasts approximately 4–6 minutes. Home history consists of chapters with a save after each one. The tutor opens the desired mission directly, regardless of the territories explored by the student. A walk around the island is not required to begin the lesson.

### Hints and results

Three levels: direction of thought → scheme or rule → analysis of one step with independent continuation. Meaningful assistance is noted in the attempt. Accidentally opening the panel before revealing the help does not count as a used hint.

A control failure is not a learning error. A training attempt is recorded after explicit verification or application. An incomplete answer does not become incorrect when exiting. Repeating an answer already shown is not considered new evidence of independence.

The result separately shows the change in the world and educational actions: independently, with a hint, after analysis, what to repeat. The completion reward is available with. Achievement for a skill requires independent application in new situations. Characters, pets, and received cosmetics do not disappear if an error occurs.

## Base Defense

The character and pets are at the base. Bubbles move along a long winding path. The student places towers, chooses a combination and location, restores energy and improves defense.

The educational purpose is to consolidate familiar short actions: calculations, fractions, percentages, equations, substitution in a formula, suitable tasks for English vocabulary and constructions. Long proofs and the first introduction to a complex topic do not fit into a quick exercise.

### Field and playing objects

Fixed top view, six sites, long route with turns, bubble entrance and base. The location determines the firing time. [Consistent visual reference](games/tower-defense/concept-reference-simplified-v2.png) is preserved. The scenery does not block the path and educational elements.

| Tower | Main role | Improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laser | Frequent damage to one target | Damage |
| Cryo | Target slowdown for other towers | Effect Duration |
| Impulse | Damage to multiple close targets | Damage radius |

Three levels of improvement. The tower only consumes energy when firing at a target. Multiple slowdowns do not stack to a complete stop: the strongest effect applies, and the time is updated. To check the balance, an accumulator of 100 and expenses of 10, 12, 25 are proposed; it is not a necessary replacement of existing numbers without passing and measuring.

| Bubble | Difference | Tactical task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | Average size and speed | Sufficient base damage |
| Quick | Small, speed mark | Positioning and Slowing |
| Large | Large, thick shell, slow | Single target damage |
| Group | Several targets nearby | Area Damage |

The color is duplicated by size or label. Enemies remain simple bubbles, with no humanoid monsters returning.

### Preparation and management

The student receives two starting building kits, selects a site and type, sees the radius, places a tower, charges it and starts the wave. The training offers a combination of laser and cryo, but the choice is free. Between waves, lineup changes and kit returns are available.

| --- | --- |
| Put | Free site, type selected, kit available; occupies the site |
| Cancel | Closes selection without wasting resources |
| Move | Between the waves illuminates the free areas |
| Replace | Between waves changes type on the selected site |
| Remove | Returns used kits between waves |
| Improve | Shows a specific stat change and consumes the available kit once |

| Goal Priority | Toggles supported target: closest to base or largest |
| Start a wave | Available with turret installed and charged |

During a wave, rearrangement and substitution are not available with explanation. The pet's cosmetic choice does not change the strength of the assistance.

### Charging and instructional feedback

The panel contains the selected tower, condition, answer field, "Check", hint and time, if enabled. The projected charge for a correct answer is 60 units with a maximum limit. In practice the wave continues more slowly; The benchmark for a short task is 12 seconds. In quiet mode, movement and the timer are stopped for the duration of the solution. The exact deceleration is determined when adjusting the balance.

| Event | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Correct answer | One charge to the selected turret, resumption of shooting |
| Wrong answer | Specific feedback, correction in the remaining time |
| Time is up | “Figure it out” or “Next task” |
| Understand | General pause and explanation |
| Solved with | Charge available, help marked separately |
| Task closed | Return to the field; an incomplete response is not considered an error |
| The wave ended during the response | The timer stops, the answer can be completed and the charge saved |
| Battery full | Charging not available with explanation |

Between waves, the student selects the desired improvement, taking into account the following composition, completes the task and receives a set. The learning action provides the resource, the strategy determines its use.

### Missions and completion

1. “First Watch”: two towers, regular bubbles, introduction to the cycle.
2. "Fast Flow": Fast targets, applying slow and positioning.
3. "Dense wave": group of targets and momentum.
4. "Big Bubble": solid target with small ones, energy distribution.
5. "Night Watch": three mixed waves with a preliminary forecast.
6. “Home Defense”: individual short chapters with repetition of a difficult skill.

Victory is to survive a given number of waves. If strength is lost, the field stops, the reason is shown and it is suggested to change the strategy. The repetition begins from the last wave, tasks receive new values. The educational history is preserved. The save must include the layout, levels, resources, wave phase and task state so that returning does not create free repeat rewards.

The tutor sees the topic, answers, help and difficulties, and can put a general pause. Control remains with the student.

## Expedition with construction

A character with a pet explores the island and overcomes obstacles with their own designs. One obstacle is a short mission; home route - multiple saved chapters. Geometric properties or mathematical dependencies determine the performance of the result.

### Cycle and materials

Inspect → choose a plan → build → predict the result → test → run a command. The start, target, obstacle, fastenings, materials, initial measurements and tools are visible. Available in spans, panels, connectors, mirrors, or transformation blocks depending on the theme. The set is always sufficient for a solution. After an error, materials are not lost.

The measurements correspond to the condition; the calculated unknown is not provided by the free tool. When drawing not to scale, this is clearly indicated. Mathematical properties are tested, not similarity to a single picture. The illustration of the operation of the bridge is not presented as a physical simulation of real building structures.

On the phone, the required control path is to select the part and press the installation location. Drag remains optional. Verification is available with the required initial actions; If a fastener or selection is missing, a specific explanation is shown.

### First mission

“Gorge Crossing”: horizontal offset 6 m, vertical 8 m, direct connection 10 m. Parts available in 8, 10, 12, 14 m. The student inspects, opens the diagram, selects a part, writes down the calculation if necessary, predicts and tests.

The 10 m piece connects the dots. When choosing 14 m, it is explained that this is the length of the walk on both sides. The hint points to a right triangle. For an earlier level, the same scene receives the task of composing the length or dimensions of a rectangular area.

### Forks and continuation

A short route may contain a more complex formation, a long route - two simple connected actions. Both check the selected skill. Constraints are visible in advance and do not change so that the chosen path becomes impossible.

A failed test shows a specific discrepancy: length, angle, area, sequence. The characters remain at the starting point. The project is saved for correction. Available options are “Correct”, “Show on diagram”, “Get a hint”, “Select another plan”.

The following missions: “Campground” - area and perimeter; “Mirror passage” - corners; "Cargo platform" - functions; “Packaging equipment” - layout and volume; “Restore map” - coordinates and scale.

Collaborative version: the tutor sees part of the drawing and requirements, the student builds. The student asks for dimensions, clarifies the relationships, shows the plan, and explains the solution. The tutor does not build for him. House chapters save already created buildings and current partial construction.

## Investigation

The student figures out what happened by selecting sources, questions, and versions to test. English trains comprehension, clarification and explanation; mathematics - dependencies, tables, time, compatibility of conditions. One short mission contains approximately three exploration points, two or three characters, and a solvable objective. A long case is broken down into episodes.

### Case Package for the lighthouse

You need to find parcel L7 with a lens. The truth of the matter is fixed before the start: the blue box was left in the workshop; Mira picked her up at 2:00 p.m., delivered her to the lighthouse at 2:15 p.m., and placed her next to the generator. There is another box with a similar number in the port.

Caretaker: “The new lens hasn’t been unpacked. Can you find parcel L7?”

1. Port: the student specifies the number, color and transfer location. An employee reports a workshop.
2. Workshop: the student finds out who took the box, when and where they were going.
3. Beacon: checks delivery, exact location and number on the box.
4. Conclusion: Identifies the item, confirms the number, and explains the path based on at least two relevant facts.

Examples of questions: “What color is the box?”, “Who picked it up?”, “When did Mira leave?”, “Where did she put it?” The acceptable order of visiting places should not destroy mandatory information or create a dead end.

### Language errors and versions

If the meaning is clear, the character answers and can gently reformulate: “Did Mira take the box?” → “Did Mira take the box?” If an error changes the meaning, he asks for clarification. A valid phrase is accepted without an exact match with the pattern. Uncertain speech recognition requires text confirmation, not a penalty.

An erroneous box causes the number to be checked; irrelevant reason - a request to find a fact confirming the location. The log and information found are saved. A lucky guess and an educated inference differ in outcome.

The student can choose the order of research and, after deciding, the method of completing the assignment. The fork changes the subsequent scene, but does not rewrite already established facts. Dialogues, log, version, available locations and current episode are saved.

Other cases: “Lost Pet” - descriptions and locations; “Night signal” - events and schedules; “Confused orders” - characteristics and quantities; “The Last Ferry” - schedule and negotiations; “Error in the archive” - contradictions; “Broken mechanism” - dependencies.

The tutor can play a witness on the card: what he knows, what he doesn’t know, what he reports immediately and after clarification. The main questions and conclusion are left to the student.

## Adventure together

General basis for repairing a station and opening a path from a closed location. The participants have different information, a common goal and a joint result. The student sees the instruments and performs the actions. The tutor sees the instructions, clarifies the symbols and conveys the rules. The teacher's speed does not determine the winner.

The final does not require pressing simultaneously in a split second. After checking the instruments and instructions, the student launches the beacon. Network latency is not a learning error.

### Escape and additional scenarios

“Closed observatory”: identify the door by description, restore the transition diagram, configure the exit. Items contain conditions, dependencies, or instructions. There is no need to look for a microscopic key in the scenery.

Other missions: “Start the freight elevator” - loads and conditions; “Radio communication with the expedition” - situation and map; "Shunting yard failure" - flow and rules; “Ferry preparation” - equipment condition and check list.

When a participant disconnects, there is a general pause. The student selects Wait, Save and Exit, or Continue with Assistant if available. The assistant has limited instructions and does not provide answers without a request for analysis. The mode is marked as independent. When the tutor returns, it is proposed to restore the joint passage; There is no hidden change of partner.

## Tutor and AI setup

Path: subject → topic → understand or consolidate or apply → suitable game → time and difficulty → viewing → launch or remote control. Instead of a map editor, a ready-made template is used.

| Button | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Select a topic | Supported learning topics |
| Pick up a game | Compatible mechanics with instructional action explanation |
| Ready set | Verified Content |
| Create an AI Variant | New set inside script restrictions |
| View assignments | Conditions, answers, solutions, tips |
| Replace task | New element of the same theme and complexity |
| Easier or More Difficult | Changing parameters before start |
| Try as a student | Viewing without result for student |
| Start lesson | Unlocks mission |
| Assign continuation | Selects short home chapters |

The tutor sees the load: for example, 6-8 short answers or one construction with an explanation. AI prepares conditions, answers, solutions, common mistakes, tips and fallback options.

| Game | Acceptable changes | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base defense | Tasks, numbers, explanations | Tower rules and map patency |
| Expedition | Dimensions, materials, restrictions | Existing and testable solution |
| Investigation | Replies, language level, characters | Consistent History and Available Basis of Inference |
| Joint renovation | Indications and instructions | Compatible Role Details and Achievable Goal |

Before launching, the existence of a solution, correctness of the answer, consistency of hints, duration, presence of all necessary data and alternative correct answers are checked. An unverified set is not shown to the student. After failure, a new generation or a ready-made package is available.

During gameplay, the AI ​​can explain a step, restate a clue, help with a question, and answer within the character's facts. He doesn't change the answer, the condition, or the key clue in the middle of an attempt. An uncertain assessment of an English answer leads to clarification or confirmation by the tutor, not to an automatic error. The started mission continues with the prepared content when the AI ​​is unavailable.

## Report and next learning task

The report shows completed learning activities, independence, assistance, repeated errors and the basis for the conclusion. Control speed is not passed off as knowledge. Confidence in short answers is important for defense; for construction - choice of method and correction; for investigation - questions and grounds; for a cooperative - description, clarification and application of the rule.

Tutor buttons: “Assign repetition” with new tasks, “Give next chapter”, “View attempt”, “Discuss error”. Game progress and educational progress are stored separately. Implementation checks and workflow are in [plan](ISLAND_GAMES_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

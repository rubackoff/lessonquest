# Runner visuals

`orbital-city-v1.png` - The original background generated by the built-in ImageGen on October 7, 2026 for this scene. Working version: `public/game-assets/orbital-runner/orbital-city.webp`, Sharp to WebP conversion with quality 86. No interface, text, characters or obstacles are applied to the background: they are added separately in the application.

Description for generation: a bright futuristic city on an orbital station, clear blue sky, soft clouds, white and silver buildings with glass and turquoise details, depth of perspective, calm gaming style; free center for a three-track track, no text, logos or characters.

Interface concepts are also generated by the built-in ImageGen and stored in `docs/games/orbital-runner`. Current landmark `concept-educational-v2.png`: white hat, large educational question, three answer options along the tracks, a light three-dimensional track and A/B/C beacons, a reading card with the beginning of the section, lower control buttons; dark blue text and turquoise accent. The first concept precedes the refinement of the training scenario.

The track, obstacles, coins and characters in the application are real Three.js objects. Characters use pre-existing generic GLBs from `public/game-assets/avatars`; this step does not change their geometry, textures, or approved running animations.
